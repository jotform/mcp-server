# App directory

ChatGPT and claude.ai list Jotform in their app directories. You connect with a few clicks. You get forms and responses.

## ChatGPT

1. Open the [ChatGPT apps directory](https://chatgpt.com/apps) and search for **Jotform**.
2. Open Jotform and choose **Connect**.
3. Sign in to Jotform.
4. Start a chat and choose Jotform.

## claude.ai

1. Open your profile menu and choose **Connectors**.
2. Find **Jotform** and choose **Connect**.
3. Sign in to Jotform.
4. Turn on the Jotform connector in your chat.

Want Sign or Apps on claude.ai? Install the [Claude plugins](claude-plugins.md).

## Check that it works

Ask:

> Find my forms.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Jotform is not in the directory | Ask your workspace admin if Jotform is allowed. |
| You are asked to sign in | Open the Jotform listing or connector and connect again. |
| You see the wrong forms | Sign in with the right Jotform account. |
